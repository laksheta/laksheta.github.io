This research proposes a fault-tolerant control (FTC) system for a single-phase DAB converter that addresses open-circuit faults (OCFs) without requiring significant hardware modifications. By transitioning from Single-Phase Shift (SPS) to Adjusted Phase Shift (APS) mode, the system handles multiple faulty switches using only one current sensor on the primary side. 

Key highlights:
* **Hardware Efficiency:** Reduces sensor requirements and avoids computationally expensive techniques.
* **Seamless Integration:** Designed for easy implementation on microcontrollers for hardware verification.
* **Versatility:** Optimized for electric vehicle charging, energy storage systems, and renewable energy integration.
