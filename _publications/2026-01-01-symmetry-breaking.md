This paper proposes an enhanced fault localization strategy for Dual Active Bridge (DAB) converters based on transient symmetry breaking. The proposed method addresses the mathematical singularity at the unity-gain boundary ($k = 1$), where primary- and secondary-side open-circuit fault signatures become indistinguishable in frequency-domain diagnostics. When a fault is detected near $k \approx 1$, a controlled adjustment of the voltage ratio and phase shift is introduced to separate the overlapping harmonic signatures, restoring fault discriminability without additional sensors or power interruption. Validation on a 7 kW DAB system demonstrates that the proposed approach expands the discriminability margin from 0.01 to 0.15 and achieves accurate fault identification with approximately 4 ms latency, providing a cost-effective software-based solution for improving DAB converter reliability across the full operating range.
