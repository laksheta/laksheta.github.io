This paper explores the implementation of Long-Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks for precise voltage control in Dual-Active-Bridge (DAB) converters. The research focuses on utilizing deep learning to improve transient response and maintain stability across varying load conditions compared to traditional control methods.
