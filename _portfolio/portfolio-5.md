## Project Overview
This research focuses on the development of a full-scale Solid-State Transformer (SST) simulation. The primary objective is to integrate the **MMC rectifier**, **DAB**, and **MMC inverter** stages into a single, high-fidelity real-time environment using **Typhoon HIL**, ensuring the system is optimized for real-time execution.
