## Project Overview
As a **PCB Design Intern** at **PT Helber Elektronika Indonesia**, I developed a unified hardware solution for wireless embedded applications. The objective was to consolidate the processing power of the Arduino Uno (Atmega328P) with the WiFi connectivity of the ESP 12-E into a single, production-ready PCB.

---

## Core Contribution

### 1. Integrated Wireless PCB Design
* **The Challenge:** Integrating two architectures into a single PCB while maintaining stable power for both the microcontroller and the high-current WiFi module.
* **My Execution:** I designed a PCB that integrates the Arduino Uno and ESP 12-E architectures. I developed a unified power management circuit to support their different voltage requirements and optimized trace routing for reliable serial communication. The result was a compact board optimized for IoT prototyping and wireless embedded systems.
