This research introduces an AI-based methodology for designing synchronous buck converters by combining machine learning models with genetic algorithms (GA). By setting strict fitness criteria for voltage ripple ($\le 1\%$), current ripple ($\le 10\%$), and component volume ($\le 7\text{cm}^3$), the AI-driven approach successfully optimized the converter parameters beyond the capabilities of conventional manual design.

**Key Results:**
* **Efficiency:** Achieved **99.383%**, surpassing the conventional design.
* **Compactness:** Reduced total component volume to **5.732 cm³** (a ~25% reduction).
* **Precision:** Achieved a voltage ripple of only **0.168%**, significantly lower than the 1.154% seen in standard methods.
